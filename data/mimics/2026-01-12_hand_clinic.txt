Referral, 12 January 2026. Riverside Family Health Clinic, Burlington, Ontario. To: Hand and Wrist Clinic.
Re: Right hand numbness and tingling, three months.

Thank you for seeing Priya Nair, a 52-year-old right-handed bookkeeper who is otherwise healthy. For the
past three months she has had numbness and tingling in her right thumb, index, and middle finger that is
worse at night and often wakes her. Shaking her hand out brings some relief. She also notices mild weakness
of her grip when opening jars.

On examination, Tinel's sign was positive at the right wrist and Phalen's test reproduced her symptoms
within thirty seconds. Grip strength was mildly reduced on the right compared with the left. Reflexes were
normal and symmetric throughout. No muscle wasting or fasciculations were seen. She has no neck symptoms
and no symptoms in the left hand or either leg. I suspect carpal tunnel syndrome, likely a median nerve
entrapment at the right wrist.

I would be grateful for your assessment and consideration of nerve conduction studies. She takes no
regular medications and has no other significant history.
