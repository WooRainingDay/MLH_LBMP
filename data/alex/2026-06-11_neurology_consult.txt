Upper limbs. Marked wasting of the right thenar eminence and first dorsal interosseous, with relative sparing of the hypothenar muscles. Fasciculations in both forearms and the right deltoid. Gait. Right foot drop with a high-stepping gait and a cane. Unable to walk on heels on the right. Romberg negative.
