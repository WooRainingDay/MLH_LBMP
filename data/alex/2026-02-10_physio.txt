Discharge summary, 10 February 2026. Westdale Physiotherapy and Sports Clinic. 12 August 2025 to 5
February 2026, 18 sessions. To: Dr. P. Raman.
Re: Right foot drop, treated as peroneal weakness. Not improving.

Alex was referred in August 2025 for a right foot that catches on curbs and slaps on landing. He had
stopped running in May because the right leg "felt heavy and would not do what I asked". A lumbar spine
MRI in December 2025 was reported as unremarkable for his age, with no nerve root compression.

Over six months of strengthening, balance work and a trial of an off-the-shelf ankle brace, his right ankle
dorsiflexion has gone from 4 out of 5 to 3 out of 5. Calf and thigh circumference on the right are each about
2 cm smaller than the left. He now uses a cane outside the house. He reports two falls, both in the evening,
one on the stairs at home and one leaving the school at dusk. He has stopped cycling to work and drives
instead.

Three things concern me. The weakness is progressing despite treatment. His reflexes are brisk, including
at the right ankle, which is not what I would expect from a peripheral nerve problem. And he mentions that
his right hand has been weak for well over a year and that a wrist operation did not help it. I do not think
this is an ankle problem. I would strongly recommend a neurology referral, and I have told Alex so.
